Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we do

Billo helps you make invoices and receipts as PDF or Word files.

Your invoices

Invoices you create are saved only on your own device (browser storage). When you download or share a file, the details are sent to our server to build the file, and they are not stored there. Clearing your browser data deletes your saved invoices.

Analytics

With your consent we use Google Analytics to count visits, countries and devices. You can decline in the banner.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card details. Your email is shared with Paystack to process the payment and send a receipt.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you may ask what data we hold or request its deletion. Contact: bigebenezer55@gmail.com
